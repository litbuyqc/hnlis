03 / Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

From a product find to a useful shortlist.

A shortlist works best when every entry answers the same practical questions.

01.

Browse with a purpose

Start with a category and an intended use. For Oopbuy spreadsheet finds, record the source link, chosen color, size and the date you checked the listing. Treat missing information as a question to resolve.

02.

Compare the exact variant

For Oopbuy shoes, inspect size conversions and foot-length guidance. For Oopbuy clothing, compare garment measurements against something you already own. Similar photos do not prove identical materials or construction.

03.

Estimate the complete cost

Before committing to Oopbuy accessories or a larger order, account for applicable item prices, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and destination charges. Confirm current amounts and terms directly.

05 / Direct answers

Before you follow
the next link.

Clear answers to common questions about this Oopbuy spreadsheet guide and the product research process.

CAFANS category pages are the browsing destinations. This page does not operate checkout, verify sellers or guarantee agent support.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a browsing and comparison guide. Its category links open CAFANS, where you can review the listings currently displayed. Use the comparison fields above to organize your own research.

How should I compare Oopbuy spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact product variant, measurements, listing photos, seller terms and estimated total cost. A product image or spreadsheet entry alone is not enough to confirm quality or authenticity.

Can any product link be used with Oopbuy?

Do not assume every link is supported. Check the current requirements in your shopping agent account and verify that the source listing and selected variant are available before paying.

What should I check when choosing a clothing size?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item that fits you. Review how measurements were taken, whether units are centimeters or inches, and whether the stated dimensions refer to the garment or the body.

Do brand names or product photos prove authenticity?

No. A name, logo, illustration or seller image does not independently establish authenticity. Review the source, seller evidence and purchase protections, and avoid treating a discovery list as authentication.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
